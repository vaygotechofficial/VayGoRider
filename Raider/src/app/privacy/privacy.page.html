Privacy Policy

VayGo Rider (Driver App) · Last updated {{ lastUpdated }}

VayGo ("we", "us", "our") operates an on-demand ride platform that connects drivers ("you") with passengers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the VayGo Rider (driver) app, how we use it, and the choices you have. By registering as a driver or using the app, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Your name, mobile number, and account details when you register.
  • Driver verification (KYC) details and documents, and vehicle details such as type and registration number, required to offer rides.
  • Payout / subscription information needed to pay you and manage your account.
  • Messages you send to passengers or to support, and any ratings or feedback.

Information collected automatically:

  • Location. With your permission, we collect your device's precise GPS location — including while the app runs in the background and you are online — so we can match you to nearby ride requests, share your live position with the assigned passenger, calculate distances and fares, and support safety features. Location is essential to driving on VayGo; without it you cannot receive or complete rides.
  • Device information (model, operating system, app version) and a push-notification token so we can send you ride offers and updates.
  • Trip records: pickup/drop points, routes, distances, fares, and timestamps.
  • Your online/offline status and availability.
  • Basic diagnostic and usage data to keep the service reliable.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and verify your driver account via one-time password (OTP) and KYC.
  • To offer you nearby ride requests and enable pickup, navigation, and drop-off.
  • To calculate fares, pickup charges, and your earnings, and to process payouts.
  • To share your live location and vehicle details with the assigned passenger.
  • To send ride offers, status updates, and service notifications.
  • To provide support, prevent fraud and misuse, ensure safety, and improve the service.
  • To comply with legal, tax, and regulatory obligations.

3. Information Shared With Others

  • Passengers. To complete a ride, we share your name, vehicle details, rating, and live location with the passenger you are matched with.
  • Service providers. We use trusted providers for maps and routing, notifications, and payments, who process data only on our behalf.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information to comply with law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of users and the public.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Location Data

Because drivers must be locatable to receive rides, the app collects your location while you are online — including in the background — and stops location-based collection when you go offline. You control location access through your device's permission settings, but you cannot take rides while it is disabled.

5. Data Retention

We keep your account, KYC, trip, and earnings information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service, process payments, resolve disputes, and meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements. You can request deletion of your account (see Contact), subject to records we must retain by law.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including secured connections and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

7. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access, correct, or update your profile and vehicle information in the app.
  • Turn location, notifications, and other permissions on or off in device settings.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated Policy.

9. Contact Us

Questions or requests about this Policy or your data? Contact VayGo support through the in-app Support section, or email support@vaygotech.com.
